# Learnings

Append one or two concise, one-line lessons here after a trade closes (a
new entry appears in `memory/trades.jsonl`). Do not append on cycles where
nothing closed.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD SYMBOL: lesson text`

Rules:
- Maximum 50 lessons in this file at any time.
- When the count reaches 50, summarize the oldest 25 into 5 entries
  (prefixed `- SUMMARY:`) and remove the 25 originals, keeping the newest
  25 individual lessons untouched. The file should then hold 30 lines
  (5 summaries + 25 recent) until it grows back toward 50.
- Lessons inform reasoning and confidence only. They must never override
  or relax a rule in `STRATEGY.md`.
