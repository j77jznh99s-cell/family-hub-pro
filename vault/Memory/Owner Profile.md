# Owner Profile

What agents should know about how the owner works. Only add things the owner has said or clearly shown.

## Goals
- Build games and apps that earn real revenue. Currently focused on Roblox, starting with [[Sproutling Isles]].
- Run a team of AI agents that keep memory, know their roles, and complete tasks on their own over time.

## How they work
- Often on a **phone** (Claude app), sometimes on a **laptop** (Roblox Studio lives there).
- Prefers in-depth research and "the best answer possible" over quick takes.
- Wants original creations: similar in feel to hits, never copies.
- Obsidian is installed but **not synced**. GitHub is the shared store.

## Communication preferences
- Short chat replies; put detail in the vault or a Claude doc.
- Plain language. Explain technical steps click by click when they need to act (for example, Roblox Studio or GitHub settings).
